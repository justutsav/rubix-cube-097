## Box 3 — How it Addresses the Problem (the flow: 5 steps → 6)

1. **VOICE-FIRST MULTILINGUAL INTAKE** — missed call, callback, her language. No literacy, no data.
2. **CONVERSATIONAL PROFILING** — education, family occupation, current livelihood, skills and interests, mobility, self-employment vs wage, local realities. Seven fields, fixed order, each read back for confirmation.
3. **DIALECT-ROBUST UNDERSTANDING** — the answer is classified over a closed set, not transcribed. Phonetic and fuzzy trade-lexicon matching plus spoken read-back, so a wrong transcript still yields a right field.
4. **NSQF ELIGIBILITY CHECK & PATHWAY MAPPING** — the gazetted entry criteria (with the schooling ↔ experience trade-off) gate the 2,814-row NQR register *before* ranking. Where she does not qualify, the system returns a **NEAR-MISS with the exact gap**, not a silent rejection.
5. **ACTIONABLE LIVELIHOOD RECOMMENDATIONS** — courses, trades, skill gaps, and region-specific employment or enterprise options, spoken back and sent as a plan she can act on.
6. **DISTRICT DEMAND → PERSPECTIVE PLAN** — every interview becomes an aggregated trade demand for the district console, in the format the officer uploads to the PM-AJAY portal, on the Ch.3 ¶9 calendar: districts by the 1st week of April, State prioritises by 15 April, forwarded by 21 April.

Step 6 is the one that answers four of the five "Basic Issues under GIA". Do not cut it for space — cut the empty white box instead.
