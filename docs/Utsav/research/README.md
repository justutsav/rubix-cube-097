Two facts that reframe everything:

1. The scheme guidelines **already mandate** that a beneficiary's interest be assessed before
   they are placed in a trade. The only instrument ever provided for it was *"publicity"*.
   This PS is a request for the missing instrument.
2. The PS's first "Basic Issue" says *"Planning of the **Perspective plans**"* — a term that
   appears **zero times** in the Feb 2022 guidelines and **seventeen times** in the May 2023
   revision. The PS is written against the 2023 document, which replaced GIA's Annual Action
   Plan with a multi-year Perspective Plan on a hard April-May calendar. See
   `01-the-customer.md` §2.4.
